Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deepe Labs, Inc. ("Deepe Labs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this corporate website, deepelabs.com (the "Site"). It does not cover our mobile applications or other products, such as Deepe, which are governed by their own separate privacy policies made available within those applications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Correspondence you send us, such as messages to hello@deepelabs.com, including whatever contact details and content you choose to include.
  • Standard technical information collected by our hosting provider when you visit the Site, such as IP address, browser type, and access timestamps, as is typical for any website.

The Site does not use cookies, does not include analytics or advertising trackers, and does not ask you to create an account or submit content beyond an optional email to us. This page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of that standard web request.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries sent to our contact email.
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who support the Site's operation, such as our hosting and DNS providers, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Deepe Labs or others.
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy or a materially similar one.

4. Data Retention

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and comply with legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 9.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.
