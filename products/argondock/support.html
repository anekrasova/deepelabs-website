Argon Dock Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem to report: email hello@deepelabs.com and we will get back to you.

Does Argon Dock need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no account. Everything runs on your device.

What does it cost?

Argon Dock is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

What devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets. The app works offline.

What happens to my data if I switch phones or delete the app?

Your timers, categories, goals, and statistics are stored only on your device. Export your statistics as CSV from the Statistics screen before switching devices or uninstalling, if you want to keep them. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Still need help?

Write to hello@deepelabs.com with your device and a description of what you're seeing.
