Argon Dock

Time you can see from across the desk.

Argon Dock is a focus timer for the phone or tablet on your desk. Choose a length, press Start, and a lamp shows the time left. It dims as the block runs down and goes out at the end.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

App Store and Google Play listings are not live yet.

A lamp, not a number. The filament shortens and dims as time runs down, so the amount left reads at a glance. Large digits beside it are readable from about three feet away, and the screen stays awake for the whole block. The time left also shows on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch, and on Android in a notification, each with Pause and Resume.

One thing at a time. Line up today's blocks in the docket, each with a title, a category and a length, and start the next one with a tap. A short break follows each run, and Repeat turns a block into Pomodoro rounds when you want them.

Quiet by design. A soft cue before a long block ends, then light, a tap and a chime that any touch stops. On Android the app can switch on Do Not Disturb for the run; on iPhone it keeps its own alerts quiet and works with your Focus through Shortcuts.

Made to sit on a desk. Five neon skins, a skin editor that takes hex colors to match your keyboard or lights, and six clock faces from the Lamp to Nixie tubes and a cuckoo clock.

Yours, on your device. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. Your sessions never leave the phone unless you export them.

How it works

  1. Put a phone or tablet on a stand at your desk.
  2. Pick a category and a length, or an open timer.
  3. Press Start and work. Look up whenever you like.
