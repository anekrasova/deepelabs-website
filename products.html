Deepe

Deepe turns a driver's walkaround video into a signed DVIR. The app checks the recording against FMCSA regulations (49 CFR Parts 392, 393, and 396) and produces a compliant inspection report, with cargo securement, tarped-load, and periodic-inspection checks built in. Deepe is in early access today, distributed directly to fleets rather than through app stores.

Learn more at deepe.com, or register your fleet to get started.
