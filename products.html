Deepe

Deepe is a pre-trip inspection and cargo security application for commercial trucking, currently in active development. It is designed to help drivers and fleets document vehicle inspections and cargo condition through photo and video capture, with backend processing built to support compliance recordkeeping. Deepe is not yet publicly released.
